# PaperConan capability attribution

CitrusQA's research-audit workflow is an independent adaptation of the publicly
documented detector categories and review boundaries of PaperConan 0.9.0:

- Project: https://github.com/zixixr/paperconan
- License: MIT (upstream `LICENSE`)
- Reviewed documentation: `docs/detectors.md`, `docs/cli.md`, and the
  `skills/paperconan/references` review rubric.

No PaperConan source files are vendored in this integration. CitrusQA keeps its
own request-scoped parsers, report formats, and session-bound artifact storage.
The audit signals are triage hints; they do not establish error, misconduct, or
author intent.
