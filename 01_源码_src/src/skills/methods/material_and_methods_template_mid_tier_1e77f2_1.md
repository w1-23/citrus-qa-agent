## 提取句式 (Abstract Templates)

### 句式 1

- **原文**: The experiment was installed in June 2006 in a citrus commercial area in the municipality of Rio Real-BA (11°29’07” S, 37°56’04” W; 179 m altitude).
- **模板**: `The [Experiment Type] was conducted in [Time Period] within a [Location Description] situated at [Geographic Coordinates].`
- **变量指南**:
  - `[Experiment Type]`: 填入如 field trial, survey, observational study 等实验类型
  - `[Time Period]`: 填入具体时间如 October 2020, the rainy season
  - `[Location Description]`: 填入如 urban residential zone, agricultural research station
  - `[Geographic Coordinates]`: 填入如 34°N, 118°W; 50 m elevation
- **语气分析**: 客观严谨，用于准确交代实验的时间、地点和空间位置，提供可复现性信息。

### 句式 2

- **原文**: The climate is classified as transitional to Aw (tropical sub-humid to dry), with an average annual temperature of 23.8° C and rainfall annual average of 919 mm, concentrated from April to October.
- **模板**: `The [Environmental Feature] is classified as [Climate Category], characterized by [Key Metric 1] and [Key Metric 2], with [Temporal Pattern].`
- **变量指南**:
  - `[Environmental Feature]`: 填入如 soil type, vegetation cover, hydrological regime
  - `[Climate Category]`: 填入如 semi-arid, temperate continental, tropical monsoon
  - `[Key Metric 1]`: 填入如 mean annual temperature of X°C, average pH of Y
  - `[Key Metric 2]`: 填入如 annual precipitation of Z mm, organic matter content of W%
  - `[Temporal Pattern]`: 填入如 concentrated from June to September, peaking in winter
- **语气分析**: 描述性且客观，用于介绍研究区域的背景环境特征，为后续讨论提供基础。
