## 提取句式 (Abstract Templates)

### 句式 1

- **原文**: A very wide distribution of leaf Cl concentrations under salinity conditions was observed as compared to that for the root.
- **模板**: `A very wide distribution of [Measured Parameter] under [Experimental Condition] was observed as compared to that for [Comparison Subject].`
- **变量指南**:
  - `[Measured Parameter]`: 填入具体测量指标，如 leaf Cl concentrations、gene expression levels、reaction rates 等
  - `[Experimental Condition]`: 填入实验条件，如 salinity conditions、high temperature、nutrient deficiency 等
  - `[Comparison Subject]`: 填入比较对象，如 the root、the control group、the wild-type 等
- **语气分析**: 客观陈述，基于观察结果进行对比，语气中立且严谨。

### 句式 2

- **原文**: These hybrids are considered salt tolerant.
- **模板**: `[Subject Entities] are considered [Adjective describing a classification or property].`
- **变量指南**:
  - `[Subject Entities]`: 填入被分类的主体，如 These hybrids、The treated samples、The mutants 等
  - `[Adjective describing a classification or property]`: 填入分类或属性形容词，如 salt tolerant、heat resistant、metabolically active 等
- **语气分析**: 肯定性判断，基于前文证据做出分类，语气自信但不过度强调。

### 句式 3

- **原文**: Moreover, similar differences in leaf Cl levels between salt-sensitive and tolerant genotypes under salinity were also found in the previous (GP) experiment.
- **模板**: `Moreover, similar differences in [Specific Indicator] between [Group A] and [Group B] under [Condition] were also found in the [Previous study or experiment label].`
- **变量指南**:
  - `[Specific Indicator]`: 填入具体指标，如 leaf Cl levels、enzymatic activity、growth rate 等
  - `[Group A]`: 填入第一组对象，如 salt-sensitive genotypes、control samples、mutant lines 等
  - `[Group B]`: 填入第二组对象，如 tolerant genotypes、treated samples、wild-type 等
  - `[Condition]`: 填入实验条件，如 salinity、drought stress、high pH 等
  - `[Previous study or experiment label]`: 填入先前实验或研究标签，如 the previous (GP) experiment、a prior investigation、our earlier work 等
- **语气分析**: 谨慎且严谨，通过引用先前实验来增强当前发现的可靠性，语气有说服力且客观。
